This site is a **discovery and tracking tool** for anime, manga and manhwa. It does not host content; it only helps you find titles, see the airing schedule and keep your own list.

## Where does the data come from?

Titles, covers, scores and the airing schedule come from the [AniList](https://anilist.co) GraphQL API. The server caches every request for an hour, which avoids needless load on AniList and keeps pages fast.

## What if AniList is down?

When the source cannot be reached, the page **does not show made-up data**. Instead you see a notice saying the data source is not responding. Failed responses are not cached, so the page recovers on its own once the source is back.

## Where is my list?

Your tracking status and favorites are stored only in your **browser** (`localStorage`). You do not need an account. If you like, you can export your list as JSON and import it on another device.

## Privacy

- Analytics, if enabled, is cookie-free.
- Fonts are bundled with the project; no third-party font requests are made.
- Trailers are not fetched from YouTube until you click "load".

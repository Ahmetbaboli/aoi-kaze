Animations bring a site to life, but for some users they mean dizziness or distraction. Operating systems offer a setting for them: **reduce motion**.

## What should you do?

1. Simplify CSS animations with the `prefers-reduced-motion` media query.
2. If you use Framer Motion, wrap the app in `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`.
3. Do not render purely decorative effects (particles, falling leaves) at all.

```css
@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) {
  *, *::before, *::after {
    animation-duration: 0.01ms !important;
    transition-duration: 0.01ms !important;
  }
}
```

## One tip

When you turn off animations that carry information (like a notification confirming a form was sent), do not lose **the information itself**. The motion goes away, the message stays.

> Good animation is the kind you do not notice, but you feel its absence.
